Growth Marketing Case Study

Building Multi-Channel Customer Acquisition

God Particles • Agency Experience

Planned and executed integrated marketing initiatives across content, social media, landing pages, and paid campaigns to improve customer acquisition, engagement, and overall marketing performance.

Overview

Working across multiple brands required more than executing individual marketing activities. Each client needed a coordinated strategy that combined multiple channels to attract, engage, and convert the right audience.

The objective was to create consistent customer experiences across different touchpoints while supporting measurable business growth.

The Opportunity

Different businesses relied on different marketing channels depending on their goals. Some focused on lead generation while others prioritized brand awareness or community engagement.

The opportunity was to connect these channels into a more cohesive marketing approach where content, paid campaigns, landing pages, and analytics worked together instead of operating independently.

How I Contributed

  • Created content strategies aligned with business goals.
  • Managed social media campaigns across multiple platforms.
  • Supported Meta advertising campaigns.
  • Reviewed landing pages for conversion opportunities.
  • Monitored campaign performance using analytics tools.
  • Prepared performance reports and optimization recommendations.

Marketing Channels

Channel Business Purpose
Content Marketing Educate and attract target audiences
Social Media Increase engagement and brand visibility
Meta Advertising Drive targeted traffic and awareness
Landing Pages Improve visitor-to-lead conversion
Analytics Measure performance and user behaviour
Reporting Identify optimization opportunities

Key Insights

Consistency Across Channels Matters

Maintaining consistent messaging across paid, organic, and social channels strengthens brand recognition and improves campaign effectiveness.

Landing Pages Influence Conversions

Even well-performing campaigns benefit from optimized landing pages that reduce friction and clearly communicate value.

Analytics Should Guide Decisions

Tracking user behaviour helps identify which channels contribute most to business objectives and where improvements are needed.

Integrated Marketing Creates Better Outcomes

Combining multiple marketing channels provides stronger customer experiences than relying on a single acquisition source.

Growth Opportunities Identified

Content

  • Create campaign-specific content.
  • Improve storytelling.
  • Repurpose high-performing assets.
  • Strengthen thought leadership.

Customer Acquisition

  • Improve audience segmentation.
  • Expand remarketing campaigns.
  • Optimize landing pages.
  • Test new campaign creatives.

Measurement

  • Build marketing dashboards.
  • Track campaign attribution.
  • Review engagement metrics.
  • Identify channel performance trends.

Key Takeaways

This experience reinforced that successful customer acquisition depends on how effectively different marketing channels work together. By combining content, paid campaigns, analytics, and landing pages into a cohesive strategy, businesses can create stronger customer journeys and more sustainable long-term growth.
