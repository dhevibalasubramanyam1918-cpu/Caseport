Growth Marketing Case Study

Optimizing Enterprise Lead Acquisition

Solinas Integrity

Supported enterprise lead generation initiatives by analysing Google Search campaign performance, evaluating landing page effectiveness, and identifying opportunities to improve acquisition efficiency through data-driven insights.

Overview

Solinas Integrity develops AI-powered robotic inspection solutions for industrial infrastructure. Their products are primarily used by municipalities, manufacturing companies, and utility providers to inspect underground pipelines safely and efficiently.

As an enterprise business with a long buying cycle, generating qualified inbound enquiries is a critical part of its digital marketing strategy.

The Opportunity

The company was running Google Search campaigns to capture users actively searching for industrial inspection solutions. My objective was to analyse campaign performance, understand user behaviour, evaluate landing page effectiveness, and identify opportunities for scalable lead generation.

How I Contributed

  • Analysed Google Ads campaign reports
  • Monitored conversion performance
  • Reviewed landing page messaging
  • Evaluated user behaviour using GA4
  • Identified growth opportunities
  • Supported campaign performance analysis

Campaign Performance

Metric Result
Impressions 22,300
Clicks 4,853
CTR 21.76%
Average CPC ₹8.12
Ad Spend ₹39,391
Conversions 651
Conversion Rate 13.41%
Cost per Conversion ₹60.51

Key Insights

Strong Search Intent

A click-through rate of 21.76% indicated that campaign messaging aligned well with user search intent, resulting in highly relevant traffic.

Efficient Lead Generation

The campaign generated 651 conversions while maintaining an average cost per conversion of ₹60.51.

Opportunity to Scale

Search Impression Share remained below 10%, indicating additional demand that could potentially be captured through campaign improvements.

Budget Constraints

Campaign diagnostics showed limitations caused by budget allocation and Ad Rank, highlighting opportunities for increased visibility.

Growth Opportunities Identified

Campaign

  • Expand keyword coverage
  • Improve Quality Score
  • Increase budget for high-performing campaigns
  • Improve ad relevance

Landing Page

  • Improve CTA visibility
  • Reduce form friction
  • Add customer proof
  • Strengthen value proposition

Analytics

  • Build GA4 funnel reports
  • Improve event tracking
  • Create Looker Studio dashboards
  • Analyse lead sources

Key Takeaways

This project reinforced the importance of evaluating the complete customer acquisition journey rather than focusing solely on campaign metrics. Analysing user behaviour alongside campaign performance and landing page effectiveness helped identify practical opportunities to improve enterprise lead generation.
